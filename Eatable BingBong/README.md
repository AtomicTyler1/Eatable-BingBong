# Eatable BingBong

You can now eat bing bong and the anicent idol!

Wait... why would you want to do that??!!??

<details> 
<summary>[SPOILERS]: Bing Bong </summary> 
- You gain infinite stamina for 15s (+1s per time cooked) 
- You gain 10% (0.1) curse damage (+10% per additional time cooked) 
- You gain drowsiness over 10s after infinite stamina wears off 
</details>

<details> 
<summary>[SPOILERS]: Ancient Idol</summary> 
- You gain infinite stamina for 10s (+1s per time cooked) 
- You gain 20% (0.2) curse damage (+20% per additional time cooked) 
- You gain 60s of invincibility 
</details>
